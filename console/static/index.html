Board

The LEDs, the opening hours and the wall screen, beside the webcam.

Now

LEDs

LEDs off: dark now and whenever idle; a cast still lights. Back to the default ends it. Both last until the listener restarts.

Brightness (of the configured level, until a restart)

100%

Day and night

Day inside the opening hours (Config → Opening hours, per day); outside them the LEDs go dark, casts are ignored and the wall screen is off.

Stay on tonight

Day mode (LEDs, casts, the wall screen) until that time, whatever the hours say: later today, or after midnight if it's already past. It ends by itself and shows on the overview until it does.

Force (NIGHT_FORCE, for tests: stays until Auto)

Wall screen

Deploy
Ship pi/ to the Pi, and what differs from the repo.

    

      
Deploy pi/ to the Pi

      
This happens on its own: every 10 minutes the laptop puts main's Pi files on the Pi (console/autodeploy.py, a scheduled task): in opening hours only after 15 minutes with nobody using the board, outside them straight away. These buttons are for doing it by hand.

      
Runs bash pi/deploy.sh: copies pi/ over and installs what changed, with no password (the Pi's /etc/sudoers.d/pb-deploy rule). A real deploy restarts the listener if its files or config.env changed (do it when the gym is closed). On a freshly flashed card without that rule, run bash pi/deploy.sh in Git Bash once instead: it asks for the password and puts the rule back.

Ship

          

          
          

            
            
            
          

          
Ship commits the ticked files (none ticked: it pushes what's committed) and pushes main, which deploys to real users, after one confirm. bash build.sh --check (the syntax check and the three-places rule) runs first, and Cloudflare runs it again: a failed build keeps the last good version live. CACHE is stamped by the build. Afterwards the live sw.js is polled until it shows the new stamp.

        2.

      3. Over the hotspot: sudo systemctl stop pb-netwatch first, or it tears the hotspot down.
        4. 
        
      4. No SSH at all: take the SD card out and read netlog.txt and netwatch.txt from its boot partition.
