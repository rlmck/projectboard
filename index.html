Get the new app on your iPhone

  1. Open Safari and go to symmetryboard.co.uk
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen
  3. Press and hold this old icon, then tap Delete (or Remove App)

Get the new app on your phone

  1. Open Chrome and go to symmetryboard.co.uk
  2. Tap Install app (or ⋮ → Install app)
  3. Long-press this old icon, then tap Uninstall

Your account, ticks and favourites are safe. They carry over to the new app; just sign in again there.
