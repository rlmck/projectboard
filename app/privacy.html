Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Project Board (symmetryboard.co.uk) is a free app for the symmetry board at The Hangout, Portland. You can browse problems, light them up on the wall and tick the ones you’ve climbed. We keep as little about you as we can. We never ask for your email, your real name, a photo or a phone number. There are no adverts and no tracking cookies, and your data is never sold.

Who’s in charge

The Hangout Climbing is in charge of the information in this app. If you have a question, want a copy of your data, or want something changed or deleted, email thehangoutclimbing@outlook.com, or ask a member of staff.

Use a nickname, not your real name

When you make an account, you pick a nickname. Use a nickname, not your real name, and don’t use your email, phone number or social media name. The app won’t accept names that look like an email, a web address or a phone number. By creating an account you agree to this.

Without an account

You can browse problems and light them up on the board without an account. Nothing about you is stored, apart from the short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) that the companies who host the app keep to run it and keep it safe.

What we keep if you make an account

  • Your nickname, and the date you joined.
  • Your passphrase, but only in a scrambled form that can’t be turned back into the words. Nobody can read it, not even staff.
  • Your ticks (which problems you’ve climbed, which way round, and when) and your favourites.
  • Problems and circuits you set.
  • Whether you’re on Ranks. This is off unless you turn it on.

Behind the scenes, the sign-in system needs an email-shaped address. The app makes up a random one that can’t receive email. It isn’t yours and it’s never used to contact you.

Who can see what

  • Everyone can see the problems and circuits you set, with your nickname on them.
  • Ranks shows your nickname, points and number of sends, but only if you turn on “Show me on Ranks” in your Profile. It’s off to start with.
  • Only you can see your ticks and favourites, and your points when you’re not on Ranks.
  • Admins (gym staff who look after the app) can see your nickname, when you joined, how many problems you’ve set and climbed, your last 10 ticks, and whether you’re on Ranks. They use this to help you, for example to check it’s really you before giving you a new passphrase. They can change your nickname if it breaks the rules. Everything an admin does to an account is recorded.

There’s no way to send messages to other people in the app.

If you lose your passphrase

Ask a member of staff. An admin can give you a new passphrase, and you’ll be signed out everywhere until you use it. If you lose your passphrase and no admin can reset it, your account is gone.

The robot check

When you sign in or make an account, a check from Cloudflare Turnstile makes sure you’re a person and not a computer program. To do that, Cloudflare looks at information about your browser and device. The app doesn’t keep any of it.

Your location

When you cast a problem, the app asks your phone for its approximate location and checks, on the phone itself, that you’re at the gym. Your location is never stored or sent to us or anyone else, and the app forgets it within a minute. If you don’t allow location access, casting still works.

On your device

Your browser keeps your sign-in, two small settings (whether you’ve closed the install banner and the welcome screen), an offline copy of the app, and a copy of the problem and circuit lists (with setters’ nicknames) so they still open without a connection. None of this is used to track you.

Who helps run the app

  • Supabase runs the database and the sign-in. The data is stored in London, UK.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site, counts visits without cookies, and runs the robot check.

We don’t share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete my account and all my data, or ask a member of staff. Your account, nickname, passphrase, ticks, favourites and points are deleted straight away. Problems and circuits you set stay on the board with your nickname, so other climbers can keep climbing them. You’re told this before you confirm. Otherwise, we keep your data for as long as you have an account.

Questions or complaints

Email thehangoutclimbing@outlook.com. If you’re unhappy with how your data has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
